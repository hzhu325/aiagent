## English Script (for CapCut international version)

November 22, 2024.
10 PM over RAF Lakenheath, Suffolk, England.
A National Police Air Service EC135 helicopter was on routine patrol.
Without warning, two unidentified objects appeared on their instruments.
Then the pilot reported something that chilled the crew:
The objects were targeting them.
Not flying nearby — actively pursuing the aircraft.
The crew immediately took emergency evasive action.
The objects matched every maneuver.
RAF Lakenheath is one of NATO's most strategically vital air bases in Europe.
It hosts US nuclear-capable fighter aircraft.
Witnesses said the objects moved in ways no known aircraft can replicate.
Both the UK Civil Aviation Authority and the Royal Air Force launched separate inquiries.
Neither agency has publicly released any findings.
No explanation has been offered to the public.
This did not happen in 1947.
Not in 1980.
This happened last November.
A trained law enforcement flight crew, over a nuclear-capable NATO installation, chased by two objects they could not identify.
What is flying over our most sensitive military sites?
And why is no one telling us?
