## Chinese Version (XiaoHongShu style)

1980年圣诞节翌日凌晨，英国皇家空军基地附近的森林里出现了一个金属发光体——美国军人亲眼追逐它穿越树林，它却每次都消失在黑暗深处，只留下辐射超标的土地。

1980年12月26日凌晨3时，驻扎于英国萨福克郡RAF伍德布里奇基地的美国空军士兵约翰·伯勒斯和比尔·布拉沙在东门外的伦德里森森林发现了异常光芒。他们起初以为是飞机坠毁，翻越围栏进入森林后，却看到了一个三角形金属体，表面散发彩色灯光，悬停于林地空地之上，完全无声。

安全主管技术军士吉姆·佩尼斯顿试图靠近并触摸了物体表面——他后来描述那种质感类似玻璃，上面刻有他从未见过的象形符号。随后，那个物体升空消失。

翌日清晨，调查人员在降落地点发现了三个清晰的圆形压痕，深嵌入土中。更令人不安的是，副基地指挥官查尔斯·霍尔特中校用盖革计数器测量后发现，该区域辐射水平是正常背景值的25倍以上——这一数据被正式记录在案。

第三晚，霍尔特中校亲率团队进入森林，全程携带录音机记录。那段18分钟的录音里可以清晰听到：那个发光体在树间穿行，向林中射出数道光束，还向地面投下了某种液态物质。这份录音与霍尔特的官方备忘录于2002年由英国国防部正式解密公开。

霍尔特至今坚称那不是任何地球技术，而英国政府的官方解释只有一句话——"对国家安全没有威胁"，却从未说明那个东西究竟是什么。

## English Version

On December 26, 1980, US Air Force soldiers at a NATO base in England chased a glowing metallic triangle through the trees — and found radiation levels 25 times above normal in the soil where it had landed.

The Rendlesham Forest incident unfolded over three nights outside RAF Woodbridge, a US Air Force installation in Suffolk, England. In the early hours of December 26, 1980, security patrolmen Airman John Burroughs and Staff Sergeant Budd Steffens spotted descending lights in the adjacent Rendlesham Forest. They crossed the perimeter fence on foot to investigate.

What they found was no downed aircraft. A glowing metallic object — triangular, silent — hovered above a forest clearing. Technical Sergeant Jim Penniston, the security supervisor who approached closest, reportedly touched its surface, describing it as smooth like glass and etched with strange hieroglyphic symbols. Then it rose silently and vanished into the treeline.

The next morning, investigators documented three circular depressions pressed deep into the soil at the landing site. Deputy Base Commander Lt. Col. Charles Halt personally measured the area with a Geiger counter: radiation levels registered more than 25 times background norms. These readings were formally recorded in an official memorandum.

On the third night, Halt led a team back into the forest with a tape recorder running. The resulting 18-minute audio — his voice audible and visibly shaken — captured soldiers tracking a luminous object moving through the trees, projecting beams of light downward, and at one point appearing to drip a molten substance to the ground.

Halt's memo and the audio recording were declassified by the UK Ministry of Defence in 2002. In the memo, Halt writes that the object "maneuvered through the trees" and "sent down a beam of light" near his feet.

The official UK government position: the incident posed "no threat to national security." No government agency ever explained the radiation. No explanation was offered for the depressions in the soil. Halt, now retired, has stated publicly that the object demonstrated technology beyond anything on Earth — and that he believes a cover-up took place.
